Supply 0.9.2 — corrections now match Premiere

  • Per-clip corrections moved beneath the LUTs. Exposure and tone now act on the decoded log source before the LUT stack, so pulling exposure down recovers highlight detail instead of adjusting an already-baked look — matching how you grade in Premiere (corrections on the clip, LUTs on an adjustment layer above).
  • Every Basic-Correction slider calibrated to Lumetri. Exposure, Temperature/Tint, Contrast, Saturation, Highlights, Shadows, Whites and Blacks were each measured against Premiere on S-Log3 and now behave the same — a value you dial here means what it means there.
  • Exposure now reads in EV (e.g. −1.00 EV), matching Lumetri's own scale.
  • White-balance eyedropper now samples the log source before the LUTs, so it neutralises the camera's cast while preserving the creative look.
