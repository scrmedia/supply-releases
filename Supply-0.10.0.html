Supply 0.10.0

  • Per-clip looks — give an individual clip its own LUT stack (e.g. one clip black-and-white while the rest of the wedding stays colour). The whole-session look stays the default; switch scope in the Look section.
  • Light / Dark / System appearance — pick your theme in Settings. The app now opens in Dark by default.
  • Redesigned Inspector — cleaner Premiere/Lightroom-style sections and consistent, tidier action buttons.
  • Reframe & viewer modes — Esc now reliably exits any viewer tool, with an on-screen badge showing the active mode.
  • Undo survives look changes — trying a different LUT no longer wipes your grading undo history.
  • Easier importing — a + button and right-click “Import Clips…” in the clip list, and the window opens at a more usable size.
  • Fixes: LUT names now display correctly after reopening a session, plus assorted polish.
